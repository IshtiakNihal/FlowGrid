## 4. Continuity & Alignment Rules for Video Preparation (Stage 2)

1. **Elevation Invariance:** All interior walking shots maintain a strict 1.55 m optical axis. Never dip or swoop unnaturally between floor and ceiling.
2. **Daylight Continuity:** The sun angle remains constant at South-East 42° elevation. Shadows in rooms with south-facing openings must point North-West across the floor.
3. **Finish Alignment:** White oak flooring plank direction runs longitudinal (North-South) continuously from the living room through the dining room and into the master suite.
4. **Transition Precision:** Each branch shot (entering an en-suite or balcony) has a designated return vector to prevent spatial disorientation in the viewer.
