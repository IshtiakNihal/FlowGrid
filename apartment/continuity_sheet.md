## 2. Lighting & Daylight Parameters

- **Time of Day:** **10:00 AM bright Dhaka morning**.
- **Sun Direction:** **South-East light angle (~42° elevation)**. Direct warm sunlight streams through the living room balcony (Balcony 1) and master bedroom balcony (Balcony 2), casting crisp, gentle shadow patterns on oak flooring.
- **Ambient Lighting:** Diffuse, high-CRI (95+) indirect illumination. All architectural recessed spotlights are 3000K warm white, recessed anti-glare architectural downlights (12W, 36° beam angle).
- **Cove Lighting:** Concealed 2700K warm-dim LED ribbons behind ceiling pelmets, fluted timber panels, and bathroom mirrors.
- **Ceiling Fans:** Practical, ultra-quiet aerodynamic timber-blade fans (Haiku / Lucci style, 54-inch matte black with oak blades) integrated into living, master, and semi-master bedrooms for genuine Dhaka climate realism.

## 4. Room-by-Room Architectural Schedule

### 4.1 Foyer & Entry (`V02`, `V03`)
- **Dimensions:** 6'-6" × 8'-0" (52 sq ft). Clear height: 9'-6".

- **Screen:** Vertical oak slatted divider screen (width 4'-0") offering acoustic and visual privacy between front door and formal living seating.
- **Floor:** Large-format warm greige porcelain slab (600×1200mm).

### 4.3 Dining Room & Handwash Niche (`V06`, `V07`)

## 5. Camera & Architectural Photography Standard

- **Camera Elevation:** Fixed eye-level **1.55 m (5'-1")** above finished floor level.
- **Focal Length:** **28mm equivalent** (moderate wide-angle, ~75° horizontal field of view) for interior shots, avoiding wide-angle barrel distortion or unnatural room elongation.
- **Horizon & Verticals:** **Level 0.0° pitch and roll**; strict 2-point architectural perspective with 100% straight vertical wall and door edges.
- **Aspect Ratio:** **16:9 landscape** (`1920 × 1080` master render resolution).
- **Exterior Backdrop:** Genuine Dhaka urban daylight view: treetops, warm ambient sunlight, mid-rise brick and white architectural silhouettes under a clear morning sky.
