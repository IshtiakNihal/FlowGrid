## 1. Brand Elements & Architecture

The FlowGrid visual identity consists of three harmonized architectural elements:

1. **The Grid & Monogram:**
   - A delicate **6×6 square grid** with a single 45° diagonal construction line, symbolizing architectural drafting, precision, and structural rigor.
   - An expressive, flowing calligraphic **'F' monogram** in FlowGrid Deep Pine (`#16463C`), with an upward-sweeping crest that breaks through the grid boundary to signify organic flow, bespoke elegance, and natural spatial harmony.
2. **The Studio Wordmark:**
   - **"FlowGrid"** set in a modern, clean geometric sans-serif (matching the studio's official mark) with tight, disciplined letter-spacing and title-case styling.
3. **The Studio Tagline:**
   - **"Interior and design"** set in a light geometric sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.16em`) in muted architectural slate (`#949492`).

## 4. Clear-Space & Minimum Size Rules

1. **Clear Space:**
   - Maintain minimum clear space equal to `0.25 × G` (where `G` is the width of the drafting grid, ~50px at master scale) on all four sides of the logo lockup.
   - No text, UI buttons, borders, or photographic focal elements may infringe on this clear space zone.
2. **Minimum Sizing:**
   - **Full Identity Lockup:** Minimum digital width **120 px** (or 32 mm print). Do not render below 120 px to ensure the "Interior and design" tagline remains crisp and legible.
   - **Compact Monogram:** Minimum digital width **24 px** (or 6 mm print). Ideal for mobile headers, compact sticky navbars, app icons, and social avatars.
   - **Favicon:** Use `favicon_32.png` or `favicon.svg` for browser tabs.

---

## 5. Usage Do's and Don'ts

- **DO** always display the 'F' monogram positioned with its drafting grid. The grid is an integral part of the studio's identity.
- **DO** use the official dark pine `#16463C` for primary brand expressions.
- **DO NOT** substitute the previous Bodoni-style serif wordmark. The official identity uses clean, modern geometric typography.
- **DO NOT** stretch, condense, rotate, or re-angle the 'F' flourish or drafting grid.
- **DO NOT** overlay the logo directly onto busy photographic textures without a dedicated neutral container or sufficient tonal contrast.
