## 6. Design specifically for Bangladesh

Use Bangla as the primary experience and design a complete English equivalent. Write natural, clear Bangla, with native review noted as pending unless a qualified reviewer actually checks it. English editorial headings can have a different treatment from Bangla. Never force Bangla into a Latin type treatment or artificially space its letters.

Create locally credible home scenarios based on the brief and research: apartment living/dining, storage, everyday cooking, easy maintenance, family routines, circulation, privacy and daylight. Consider heat, humidity, ventilation, ceiling fans, AC placement, window grilles and balconies where the specific room warrants them. These are design considerations, not features every Bangladeshi home must have.

Avoid default foreign penthouses, enormous mansion rooms, implausible glazing, endless marble, extravagant chandeliers and generic tropical-resort interiors. Local relevance should come from believable spaces and daily use, not decorative national symbols. Keep materials and joinery plausible; do not claim local availability or quote a price without checking a supplier.

Prioritise comfortable mobile use, readable text, visible actions and manageable media. Allow phone/WhatsApp enquiry without requiring email or an account. Use ৳/BDT for approved monetary content and familiar area units where verified. Do not assume nationwide service or publish an unverified address or telephone number.

## 8. Art direction and anti-AI-slop requirements

Starting direction: warm paper, deep pine, architectural photography, restrained serif Latin display, readable Bangla and deliberate editorial spacing. Evaluate it against actual FlowGrid assets before finalising.

Starting tokens:

- Paper `#F4F1E8`; pine `#183B35`; mist `#DEE7E2`.
- Secondary text `#56645E`; clay accent `#895239`; white `#FFFFFF`.
- Bodoni Moda 500 for large Latin display; Manrope for Latin UI/body; Noto Sans Bengali for Bangla. Check availability and document substitutions.
- Body around 18px; comfortable Bangla line-height around 1.75; controls at least 16px.
- Desktop: 12-column grid, maximum content width 1312px at a 1440px frame. Mobile: 4 columns with approximately 20px gutters.
- Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128px. Mostly square media and restrained control corners.

Do not default to gradient blobs, glassmorphism, pill-shaped everything, repetitive bento cards, floating ornaments, stock icons for every sentence, fake five-star proof or interchangeable “luxury redefined” copy. Avoid endless empty space that buries useful content. Every section needs a distinct purpose and every major image must earn its place.

Create two focused homepage composition studies within this identity, compare them against the brief, select the stronger one and refine it. This is an internal design decision; do not stop for approval unless a material brand conflict emerges. The final static composition must work before motion is added.

## 10. Design and demonstrate the animations

Animation is a required part of this assignment. Extend the original restrained motion specification with **one signature hero sequence and one project transition**, while keeping everyday controls quick and calm. Do not merely write “add smooth animation.”

Explore an architectural image-mask reveal and coordinated whole-line text entrance for the opening, plus a coherent project-image-to-detail transition. Adapt to FlowGrid’s original composition; do not copy a reference sequence. Essential hero text and contact actions must remain available without waiting for media or a decorative loader.

Keep native scrolling. Avoid scroll hijacking, compulsory long loaders, cursor followers, constant parallax, looping marquees and per-character Bangla animation. All important information must work without motion or hover.

For every meaningful interaction, document trigger, start/end states, affected layers, duration, easing, delay, interruption behaviour, mobile treatment and reduced-motion alternative. Useful starting timings: hover 150ms, menu 220ms in/160ms out, accordion 180ms, image entrance 360ms and a restrained signature sequence around 500–800ms. These are proposals to test, not measured values from reference sites.

Build native Figma prototype interactions wherever supported. For browser-specific behaviour Figma cannot faithfully reproduce, provide an annotated storyboard with key frames and exact specifications, explicitly labelled as a simulation or implementation requirement. Reduced motion should preserve immediate content and use instant changes or a brief opacity transition when appropriate.
