## 1. Provenance Policy & Editorial Standards

1. **Explicit Concept Disclosure:**  
   Every visual representation derived from architectural AI visualization is strictly labeled with:  
   - **Bengali:** `কনসেপ্ট ডিজাইন · AI ভিজ্যুয়ালাইজেশন · বাস্তবায়িত প্রকল্প নয়`  
   - **English:** `Concept Design · AI Visualization · Unbuilt Exploratory Study`  
2. **Zero Fictitious Attribution:**  
   No fabricated client names, manufactured testimonials, unverified completion dates, speculative budgets, or fictitious delivery guarantees are ever attributed to exploratory concepts.  
3. **Bangladesh Spatial Plausibility:**  
   All architectural interior studies reflect genuine Dhaka residential typologies (standard 10-foot floor-to-ceiling heights, reinforced concrete column grids, monsoon-resilient seasoned joinery, south/south-east cross-ventilation, and balcony daylight buffering).  
4. **Third-Party Separation:**  
   Assets from external channels (such as *Rumi's Fashionable House* or *Onekta Product*) remain strictly segregated unless explicit ownership, client release, and commercial licensing are formally documented.
